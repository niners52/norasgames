📓 Things only you know

About the art

Every monster, every character card, the whole map of Hawkins and all six game screens were drawn with code — shapes, gradients and glow, no photos. The Demogorgon's head is seven petals drawn in a loop.

About the quizzes

The personality quiz used to give everyone Eddie, because ties always picked the first name on the list. Now ties are broken by a coin flip. It was tested 400 times to make sure.

About Vecna's List

It isn't random. Your name is turned into a number, and that number picks your fear and your song — which is why you get the same reading every time, and your friend gets a different one.

About the campaign

There are eight different endings to The Vale of Shadows. Two of them are only possible if you pick up the right item early — and one of them you win by asking the monster a question.
