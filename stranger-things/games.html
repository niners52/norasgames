Mini Games

Three games from the Palace Arcade. Each one opens on its own page so it loads fast. 🕹️

🧇

Eggo Catch

Waffles are falling over Hawkins. Catch them in Eleven's basket and dodge the Demodogs.

Move with your finger, mouse, or arrow keys.

 💡

Light Code

Will is spelling a message through the Christmas lights. Watch the blinks, then repeat them back.

Tap the bulbs in the same order. It gets longer every round.

 🕰️

Escape Vecna

The clock is chiming and you're stuck in the trance. Hit your favorite song's beats to wake yourself up.

Tap the glowing notes before they reach the line.
