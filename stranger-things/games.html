Mini Games

Six games from the Palace Arcade. Each one opens on its own page so it loads fast. 🕹️

🧇

Eggo Catch

Waffles are falling over Hawkins. Catch them in Eleven's basket and dodge the Demodogs.

Move with your finger, mouse, or arrow keys.

 💡

Light Code

Will spells real words through the Christmas lights, like RUN, EGGO and HAWKINS. Watch the blinks, then spell it back.

Tap the bulbs in the right order. The words get longer every round.

 🕰️

Escape Vecna

The clock is chiming and you're stuck in the trance. Hit your favorite song's beats to wake yourself up.

Tap the glowing notes before they reach the line.

 🚲

Demogorgon Chase

Ride your bike down Mirkwood at night while something with no face gains on you. Grab Eggos to buy yourself room.

Swipe left and right, or use the arrow keys.

 🔦

Upside Down Maze

You're on the wrong side and your flashlight only reaches so far. Find three lost lights, then find the gate home.

Swipe, use arrow keys, or tap the buttons. Each level gets bigger.

 🔐

Break the Russian Code

Be Robin in the back of Scoops Ahoy. Turn the cipher wheel until the gibberish turns into actual words.

Six transmissions, each one harder than the last.
